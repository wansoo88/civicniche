Can't I just pull this myself?

You can — the source is public, and for a few hundred records it's easy. The full set is where it gets tedious: openFDA's skip paging caps out around 25,000 records, and the same establishment shows up across dozens of listing documents. Assembling everything means slicing by country to get past the paging cap, then deduping 65,049 registrations down to 7,130 unique manufacturers. That's the work I've already done.
